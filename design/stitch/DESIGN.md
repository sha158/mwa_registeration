## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a serene, trustworthy, and welcoming civic atmosphere tailored for youth academic competitions. It strips away traditional decorative overload—bypassing ornamental arabesques and heavy calligraphy—in favor of structural clarity, warmth, and institutional poise. The visual language merges modern architectural geometry with an earthy, scholarly warmth.

Key brand attributes:
- **Trustworthy & Protective:** High-security document verification, explicit privacy cues, and definitive structural alignments reassure guardians and young participants handling identification data.
- **Approachable & Scholarly:** Sand-toned surfaces and rich emerald foundational tones create an organic, dignified setting reminiscent of classic manuscript paper bound in dark linen.
- **Effortless Clarity:** Form-centric friction is reduced through deliberate spacing, large tap zones (minimum 48px), and persistent hierarchy.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Refined Warm Modernism**: crisp border-defined surfaces, tactile subtle tinting, absence of dense drop-shadows, and spacious typographic craft that keeps attention focused entirely on entry validation and verification status.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative yet hospitable tone through high-contrast natural hues.

### Primary & Brand Colors
- **Primary Emerald (`#0D4738`)**: Anchors critical primary action buttons, active navigation markers, and header branding.
- **Deep Cypress (`#083328`)**: Used for high-emphasis iconography, pinned headers, and brand accents requiring superior contrast.
- **Warm Ochre / Gold Accent (`#C59B27`)**: Applied strictly for step highlights, awards markers, focus rings, and capacity milestones. Never used for long-form reading text.

### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FBF9F5`)**: A soft ivory parchment that eliminates clinical glare.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean white surface dedicated to form cards, summary tables, and active inputs.
- **Surface Muted (`#F4EFE6`)**: A grounded sand hue for disabled fields, step connectors, and inactive segment indicators.

### Functional & Semantic Roles
- **Verified / Confirmed / Available**: Deep Green text (`#166534`) on Light Sage container (`#DCFCE7`). Bordered by `#BBF7D0`.
- **Pending / Capacity Warning**: Dark Amber text (`#B45309`) on Warm Sand-Amber container (`#FEF3C7`). Bordered by `#FDE68A`.
- **Closed / Alert / Invalid**: Muted Brick Rose text (`#991B1B`) on Soft Rose container (`#FEE2E2`). Bordered by `#FECACA`.

### Text & Linework
- **Primary Text (`#111827`)**: Deep slate for maximum legibility on ivory and white.
- **Secondary / Supporting Text (`#4B5563`)**: Balanced neutral for metadata and persistent form labels.
- **Dividers & Structural Borders (`#E5E7EB` / `#D1D5DB`)**: Clean hairline definitions that create spatial order without visual clutter.

## Typography

The type scale is driven entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its crisp, geometric open counters, humanist friendliness, and stability in form-heavy contexts.

### Typographic Principles
- **No Floating Placeholders:** Input labels are treated as structural headers (`label-lg`) positioned statically above input controls. Subtext or formatting hints stay pinned beneath inputs (`body-sm`) to avoid cognitive shift during typing.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Stepper figures, slot counters, and verification digits are set with tabular spacing (`tnum`) to eliminate micro-shifts during dynamic updates.
- **Hierarchical Anchors:** Section headers inside multi-stage registration flows employ `headline-sm` with a sharp contrast against `body-md` explanatory text, preventing reading fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **8-point spatial grid** combined with a fixed-width container logic for desktop forms to preserve line length and entry rhythm.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column layout. Margin is `1rem` (16px), gutters are `1rem`. Input controls stretch edge-to-edge within the padded frame. All form controls guarantee a minimum 48px vertical hit zone.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Canvas margins expand to `2rem`. Two-column form splits appear for related inputs (e.g., First Name / Last Name, Date of Birth / Gender).
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum content envelope of `840px` for the registration form flow and `1120px` for administrative dashboards. Centered alignment maintains focus and minimizes horizontal eye travel.

### Flow & Spacing Rhythm
- **Field Groups:** Vertical space between adjacent inputs within a logical sub-section is `space-lg` (24px).
- **Sub-Sections:** Spaced by `space-xl` (32px), separated by a subtle 1px divider line (`#E5E7EB`).
- **Touch Target Integrity:** Every button, selector, checkbox wrapper, and file upload dropzone maintains at least `space-xl` (48px) total clearance.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system intentionally rejects heavy raster drop-shadows and skeuomorphic lighting. Visual depth is established through **tonal stratification and architectural outlines**.

### Stratification Model
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Warm Parchment (`#FBF9F5`). Structural underlay.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of `#E5E7EB`. This creates high-contrast boundaries against the parchment backdrop.
3. **Level 2 (Floating Action Elements / Modals / Sticky Footers):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a restrained hairline border (`#D1D5DB`) and an ultra-subtle tinted grounding shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(13, 71, 56, 0.06)`. The emerald tint integrates the shadow into the palette rather than letting it turn ashen gray.
4. **Level 3 (Focused & Active Inputs):** Pure `#FFFFFF` supported by an outer halo ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.25)` and a sharp primary border of `#0D4738`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Balanced Structural (`2`)** roundedness posture. It provides sufficient curvature to feel warm and hospitable to young scholars, while retaining crisp structural authority for institutional compliance.

- **Base Radius (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied to form input fields, selection tiles, alert callouts, and administrative data tables.
- **Container Radius (16px / `1rem`):** Applied to registration phase step cards, summary card blocks, and document drop boxes.
- **Pill Radius (Full / 9999px):** Reserved strictly for compact metadata tokens: stage counters, slot capacity indicators, and verification status badges. Never applied to primary rectangular action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Emerald background (`#0D4738`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, 8px border radius. Padding: 14px 24px (minimum 48px height). Hover state shifts to Deep Cypress (`#083328`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pure white background, 1.5px border (`#D1D5DB`), charcoal text (`#1F2937`). Hover introduces a sand tint (`#F4EFE6`) with primary emerald border (`#0D4738`).
- **Sticky Mobile Action Bar:** Fixed to the mobile viewport bottom. White background with a top 1px border (`#E5E7EB`), soft emerald ambient elevation, housing a dual layout: a secondary 'Back' step button and a full-flex primary 'Save & Continue' trigger.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Standard Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#D1D5DB`, border radius 8px, padding 0 16px. Typography: `body-md` in `#111827`. Focus state shifts border to `#0D4738` with an Ochre ring glow (`rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.2)`).
- **Persistent Labels:** Styled with `label-lg` in `#1F2937`, positioned 8px above the control. Optional indicator formatted in `#4B5563` (`body-sm`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px x 20px squares/circles with 2px solid border (`#D1D5DB`). Checked state fills with `#0D4738` displaying a white checkmark or inner dot. Hit target extends to a 48px padded touch zone wrapper.

### Slot Capacity Counter
- A pill badge combining an emerald or amber indicator: `8 / 15 Teams Registered`.
- Available State: Background `#DCFCE7`, text `#166534`, dot `#15803D`.
- Limited / Near Capacity (>= 80%): Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, dot `#D97706`.
- Full State: Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#991B1B`, dot `#DC2626`.

### 4-Step Registration Progress Bar
- Linear segmented progress line displaying: `1. Member 1` → `2. Member 2` → `3. Member 3` → `4. Review`.
- Completed steps display a solid emerald circular badge (`#0D4738`) with a white checkmark, linked by a solid `#0D4738` track.
- Active step displays an Ochre ring badge (`#C59B27`) with bold white numeral on `#0D4738` fill.
- Upcoming steps display a neutral sand badge (`#F4EFE6`) with slate text (`#4B5563`) linked by `#E5E7EB` tracks.

### Document Upload Box (Aadhaar / ID Verification)
- Dashed 1.5px border (`#C59B27` when hovered, `#D1D5DB` default) on `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF9F5` base.
- Includes a prominent **Privacy & Encryption Badge**: A secure padlock icon paired with the copy *"Government ID data is encrypted and used solely for age/identity verification under event safety protocols"*, styled with an emerald tint.
- File state preview card: Shows truncated filename, file size, thumbnail/icon, and an instant "Remove / Replace" text button.

### Review & Summary Cards
- Segmented summary surfaces wrapping each member's data prior to submission.
- Header row contains the member label (`headline-sm`) along with an inline "Edit" link button that jumps back to that specific registration step.
- Grid-based key-value list pairing bold labels (`label-md`) with user-provided answers (`body-md`).

### Admin Verification Badges
- Compact pill-shaped status tags:
  - **Verified / Approved:** `#DCFCE7` fill, `#166534` text, leading check icon.
  - **Under Review:** `#FEF3C7` fill, `#B45309` text, leading clock icon.
  - **Disqualified / Action Required:** `#FEE2E2` fill, `#991B1B` text, leading alert icon.
